Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 13-04-2026

EasyConvert respects your privacy. This app is designed to function entirely on your device without collecting or transmitting any personal data.

Data Collection

EasyConvert does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • No user accounts
  • No background data collection

File Access

The app only accesses files that you explicitly select or drag into the app.

  • Files are processed locally on your device
  • No files are uploaded to any server
  • No file contents are stored after conversion

Data Usage

All processing happens entirely on your Mac. Your data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

EasyConvert does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, or external APIs.

Security

Because all operations are performed locally, your files remain under your control at all times.

Children’s Privacy

EasyConvert does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, updates will be reflected within the app listing.
